Dreamway Labs builds small, fast iPhone apps that each do a single thing well and then stop — no cluttered dashboards, no five-screen welcome tours, no subscription traps, and nothing leaving your device that does not have to.

No accounts · No trackers · No servers · No ads

How we build

01 One job, or none

We ship something when it does a single thing better than the alternatives, and then we stop adding to it. Long feature lists are how good software quietly becomes bad.

02 The device is the server

Work happens on your iPhone. Nothing to sign up for, nothing uploaded, nothing sitting on a server of ours waiting to be breached. It is a constraint we design around.

03 Pricing without tricks

No weekly plans, no paywall that appears the moment you try to export, no cancellation buried three menus deep. You should always know what you are paying for.

The studio

Dreamway Labs LLC is an independent software studio. We are small on purpose — it is the only way we know to ship software that stays simple after the first release.

What we make
Focused utilities for iPhone. Tools you open, use and close, rather than platforms that expect to live on your home screen forever.
How we pay for it
People buy the software. There are no investors waiting on growth, no advertising business that needs your attention, and no data to sell to anyone.
Where your data goes
Nowhere. Our apps process what you give them on your own device, which means there is no account to create and no copy of your files on our side.
Who you are writing to
Email reaches the studio directly rather than an outsourced support desk, and what comes in is what shapes the next release.
